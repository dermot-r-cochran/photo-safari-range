Camp

The driver drives, stops when the spotter calls, and moves on · left and right arrows (or A and D) ask him for closer or back · Space raises the camera and fires it · with the camera up the arrows move the aim · [ and ] change the shutter · Tab picks another animal in the frame · Z zooms · Esc packs up.

Asked

    About

    A wildlife-photography arcade in one page. The rule is the field's rule: the shutter follows the animal, not the light meter. Placeholder silhouettes stand in until the sprites arrive. Written by a novice for other novices; the thresholds are one camera's.

    The lessons behind it are in Photo Safari, the tutorial. Source and licence on GitHub.
