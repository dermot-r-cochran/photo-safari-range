Camp

The driver drives and stops when the spotter calls; at the stop the camera comes up by itself on what was called · A and D (or the left and right arrows with the camera down) ask him for closer or back, and put the camera down to move on; hold a key past a second and you are hurrying him, which spooks the shy ones · Space fires; it also raises the camera if it is down · with the camera up the arrows or the mouse move the aim · the mode dial turns through S, A and M (M) · the wheels set the shutter ([ and ]) and the aperture (, and .) · B rests the camera on the beanbag, or the tripod on foot · the camera focuses on what is under the centre of the frame, and Tab picks another animal in it · Z zooms · C puts the camera down without moving · N skips to the next part of the day · Esc packs up.

    About

    A wildlife-photography arcade in one page. The rule is the field's rule: the shutter follows the animal, not the light meter. The animals on the range are drawn shapes, a map rather than a picture; the photographs after a shot are the author's own. Written by a novice for other novices; the thresholds are one camera's.

    The lessons behind it are in Photo Safari, the tutorial. Source and licence on GitHub.
